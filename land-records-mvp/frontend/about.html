About This Project

Project Goal

This is an MVP (Minimum Viable Product) built to validate whether a unified land records search interface adds value for property buyers in Delhi, starting with Greater Kailash (I, II, III) and Chitranjan Park.

Data Sources

Property data comes from multiple sources:

  • Manual Entry: Data manually entered by administrators from verified documents
  • Public Listings: Information from public real estate listings (99acres, MagicBricks, etc.)
  • Crowdsourced: User-contributed data from property owners
  • Manual Portal Query: Data obtained through individual queries to official DLRC portal

Methodology

We collect property information from legal and ethical sources, anonymize personal details, and present them in a user-friendly format. Each record shows its source and last verification date.

Important Disclaimer

This data is for informational purposes only. Always verify property information through official government portals before making any decisions.

Official portals:

  • Delhi Land Revenue Corporation (DLRC)
  • NGDRS - Property Registration

Limitations

  • Data accuracy depends on source quality and verification date
  • Owner names are anonymized for privacy
  • Market value estimates are indicative only
  • Database is limited to GK (I, II, III) and CR Park areas
  • Not a substitute for official title searches or legal advice

Privacy Policy

We take privacy seriously. All owner names are anonymized. We do not collect or store personal information without explicit consent. User-contributed data is submitted voluntarily.
